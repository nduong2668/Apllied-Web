Senior Full-Stack Engineer

Reference number: FS001

Architect and deploy real-time transit telematics interfaces and microservices using React, Node.js, and high-performance WebSocket streams.

Salary and Reporting Line

Salary
AUD 7,000 - 10,000 per month, depending on experience
Reports to
Head of Smart Transit & Urban Mobility
Location
Melbourne / Singapore (Hybrid)

Key Responsibilities

  • Architect responsive telematics dashboards for municipal transit operators using React.
  • Build high-throughput microservices for real-time vehicle GPS tracking with Node.js.
  • Integrate WebSocket streams for live passenger information systems.
  • Review code and mentor junior full-stack developers in spatial data handling.
  • Work closely with city planners and product owners to translate requirements into features.

Requirements

Essential

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or related field.
  • 5+ years of professional full-stack web development experience.
  • Strong proficiency in JavaScript (ES6+), React, and Node.js.
  • Experience handling real-time data streaming and WebSockets.

Preferable

  • Experience with TypeScript and GIS mapping libraries (Leaflet / Mapbox).
  • Familiarity with containerised deployment via Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Prior work in intelligent transportation or telematics systems.

DevOps & Cloud Architect

Reference number: DO017

Engineer secure, fault-tolerant cloud infrastructures and automated CI/CD pipelines governing critical smart grid SCADA and urban services data.

Salary and Reporting Line

Salary
AUD 8,000 - 11,500 per month, depending on experience
Reports to
Head of Critical Infrastructure & Security
Location
Melbourne / Singapore (Hybrid)

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and maintain automated CI/CD pipelines for smart grid and urban data services.
  • Architect fault-tolerant cloud infrastructure on AWS/GCP using Terraform (IaC).
  • Operate and scale high-availability Kubernetes clusters for critical SCADA telemetry.
  • Enforce strict zero-trust security controls across municipal cloud endpoints.
  • Lead incident response and post-incident reviews for high-reliability infrastructure.

Requirements

Essential

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, IT or a related field.
  • 5+ years of experience in DevOps, Cloud Engineering, or Systems Architecture.
  • Hands-on expertise with Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, and Linux administration.
  • Strong knowledge of cloud network security and encryption standards.

Preferable

  • AWS or GCP Solutions Architect / DevOps Professional certification.
  • Experience with Grafana, Prometheus, and IoT telemetry monitoring tools.
  • Understanding of industrial SCADA systems or smart utility networks.

Smart City IoT & Data Engineer

Reference number: DT023

Develop streaming data pipelines for municipal sensor fleets, microgrid battery storage systems, and urban digital twin visualization dashboards.

Salary and Reporting Line

Salary
AUD 7,500 - 11,000 per month, depending on experience
Reports to
Head of Energy & Environmental Analytics
Location
Melbourne / Remote

Key Responsibilities

  • Build streaming data pipelines ingesting telemetry from thousands of municipal IoT sensors.
  • Develop data models for microgrid battery storage optimization and energy analytics.
  • Integrate spatial data APIs with 3D urban digital twin visualization dashboards.
  • Monitor sensor data quality and automate automated anomaly detection workflows.
  • Collaborate with environmental scientists and city councils on carbon-reduction metrics.

Requirements

Essential

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Engineering, Computer Science or related field.
  • 3+ years of professional experience in data engineering or IoT systems.
  • Strong Python skills and hands-on experience with Apache Kafka, Spark, or SQL.
  • Experience building robust ETL pipelines and handling time-series data.

Preferable

  • Familiarity with MQTT, CoAP, or edge computing protocols.
  • Experience with Digital Twin software platforms or 3D geospatial rendering.
  • Knowledge of renewable energy systems and smart grid data structures.
