Acknowledgement of Country & Indigenous Educational Inclusion

Pacific Alliances respectfully acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of Country throughout Australia and recognizes their continuing connection to land, waters, culture, and community. We pay our deepest respects to Elders past, present, and emerging.

As an Educational Technology company, we believe that education is the foundation of equity, voice, and opportunity. We are passionately committed to closing the digital education divide in remote and regional communities by engineering bandwidth-resilient offline learning platforms, supporting Indigenous-led digital curriculum initiatives, and promoting digital literacy.
